COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Commercial Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into on February 1, 2025, by and between Beacon Commercial Properties LLC ("Landlord"), and Horizon Retail Partners LLC ("Tenant").

SECTION 1. LEASED PREMISES
Landlord hereby leases to Tenant and Tenant leases from Landlord the commercial retail premises designated as Suite 204, located at 450 Commerce Boulevard, Chicago, Illinois (the "Premises"), comprising approximately 2,200 square feet.

SECTION 2. TERM AND AUTOMATIC RENEWAL
2.1 Initial Term. The initial term of this Lease shall be for a period of twenty-four (24) months, commencing March 1, 2025 and expiring February 28, 2027.
2.2 Automatic Renewal. This Lease shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month terms unless Tenant delivers written notice of non-renewal to Landlord at least sixty (60) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term.

SECTION 3. RENT AND SECURITY DEPOSIT
3.1 Monthly Rent. Tenant agrees to pay Landlord monthly base rent of $3,500 per month, due and payable on the first day of each calendar month.
3.2 Late Fee. If rent is not received by Landlord by the fifth (5th) day of the month, Tenant shall pay a late fee penalty of five percent (5%) of the monthly base rent ($175).
3.3 Security Deposit. Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of $5,000 as security for the faithful performance of all terms and covenants of this Lease.

SECTION 4. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
4.1 Landlord Obligations. Landlord shall maintain and repair the roof, structural exterior walls, and primary building foundations at Landlord's expense.
4.2 Tenant Obligations. Tenant shall keep and maintain the interior of the Premises, HVAC systems, plumbing fixtures, and electrical wiring in clean, sanitary, and good working order.

SECTION 5. USE AND RESTRICTIONS
Tenant shall use the Premises strictly for commercial retail office and administrative business operations and for no other purpose without Landlord's prior written consent. Tenant shall comply with all municipal codes and safety ordinances.

SECTION 6. GOVERNING LAW
This Lease shall be governed, construed, and enforced in accordance with the laws of the State of Illinois. Jurisdiction shall lie exclusively in Cook County, Illinois.
